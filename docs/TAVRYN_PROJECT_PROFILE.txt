PROJECT NAME
Tavryn

TRACK
RWA & Business Workflows

CHALLENGE
BitSafe Challenge (Contribution Pool: Decentralizing Apps on Canton)

TAGLINE
Every invoice, financed once.

ELEVATOR PITCH
Tavryn stops the same invoice from being financed twice. Suppliers offer an approved invoice to several lenders in private; the first lender to pay wins, and everyone else is simply told it's taken, never who won, never at what price. The network itself refuses a second payment, so lenders get the guarantee without showing each other their books.

THE PROBLEM
The same invoice can be financed by several lenders, because each lender sees only its own deals. A shared list of invoices would stop it, but would expose every lender's clients and prices.

WHY NOW
In September 2025 First Brands collapsed owing US$2.3bn to invoice financiers; its restructuring chief is investigating whether the same receivables were factored more than once (Global Trade Review, 30 Sep 2025). The same month, US prosecutors charged Tricolor's executives over double-pledging collateral to multiple lenders. Global factoring passed EUR 4 trillion a year in 2025 (FCI).

THE SOLUTION
Lenders compete privately for a buyer-approved invoice, and the Canton network accepts exactly one payment for it.

WHY CANTON
Canton lets lenders share one fact (whether an invoice is still available) without sharing their clients, prices or deals.

HOW IT WORKS
1. The supplier creates an invoice; only the supplier and buyer see it.
2. The buyer approves it. An invoice number can only be approved once.
3. The supplier offers it privately to several lenders.
4. The first lender to pay wins; the others are told it's no longer available.
5. The buyer repays the winner on the due date.

WHAT THE LOSING LENDER SEES
Its own offer, and: "This invoice is no longer available."

WHAT THE LOSING LENDER NEVER SEES
Who won, the amount, the price, or any other lender's business.

DECENTRALIZED BY DESIGN (BITSAFE)
The network's rules (which lenders may join, and how much of an invoice they may advance) are signed by all of the network's admins. A change needs two of three to agree; one admin alone is refused.

MEASURED
45 of 45 simultaneous payment races on LocalNet and the HackCanton DevNet: exactly one winner, every second lender refused by the network itself. Losing lender told in 0.75 s (median, LocalNet). Every duplicate approval refused. Canton Coin funding and repayment 5 of 5.

WORKING TODAY
- Live on the HackCanton DevNet: two lenders paid for the same invoice at the same moment; the network accepted one and refused the other. Rule changes were refused with one approval and applied with two.
- Real Canton Coin payments and repayments, with interrupted payments finished automatically and repayments never sent twice.
- A web app with a page for each company, and a recorded walkthrough of the full flow.

WHO USES IT
Large buyers running supplier-finance programmes, their suppliers, and the lenders funding them.

WHO PAYS
Lenders pay per invoice financed, because double financing is their loss.

CLOSING LINE
Every invoice, financed once. Prove it without revealing it.
