TAGLINE
Every invoice can be pledged once. Prove it without revealing it.

ELEVATOR PITCH
Every invoice can be pledged once. Prove it without exposing competing bids. Tavryn stops the same invoice from being financed twice—enforced by the Canton ledger. A lender offered an invoice sees that invoice's terms, but never another lender's offer, price, or win. The current model exposes which lenders were eligible for the same invoice; removing those names is roadmap work.

THE PROBLEM
The same invoice can be financed by several lenders because each lender sees only its own deal. A shared registry could stop this, but it would expose every lender's clients, prices, and volumes.

THE SOLUTION
Tavryn lets lenders compete privately for a buyer-approved invoice while the Canton ledger allows only one lender to fund it.

WHY CANTON
Canton lets lenders share one fact—whether an invoice is still available—without sharing their clients, prices, volumes, or deal terms.

HOW IT WORKS
A buyer approves an invoice. The supplier sends private offers to multiple lenders. The first lender funds it, consuming the invoice's one-use funding state. Every later attempt fails.

WHAT THE LOSING LENDER SEES
Its own offer and: "This invoice is no longer available."

WHAT THE LOSING LENDER DOES NOT SEE
The winning lender, funded amount, price, terms, receivable, clients, or volumes.

WORKING PROOF
Financier A funds successfully. Financier B's real ledger submission fails. B's party-scoped view contains none of A's offer, financed invoice, or private invoice details. The complete Daml lifecycle and role-scoped backend run on LocalNet, with recorded Canton update IDs for funding and repayment.

WHY NOT A SHARED DATABASE?
A shared database needs an operator who sees every financier's book, and no financier will hand its client list to a rival or a startup. Tavryn provides the guarantee without exposing the books.

WHO USES IT
Large buyers running supplier-finance programmes, their suppliers, and the financiers funding those programmes.

WHO PAYS
Financiers pay per invoice financed because they bear the risk and cost of double financing.

CURRENT STATUS
The single-financing mechanism, real Canton Coin funding and repayment (two-step, non-atomic, reconciled by tracking ID), threshold governance over the network rules, and a recorded browser click-through are reproducible on the selected Splice-backed VPS LocalNet. DevNet deployment and customer interviews remain in progress.
